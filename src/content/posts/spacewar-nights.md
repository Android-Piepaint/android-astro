# Intro

3 years ago, I brought a unique phone </br>
Glyph lights and Industrial style, </br>
Dot matrixs and designs was good to me </br>
But suddenly it is EOLed,
No more updates and no more fun, </br>
It's time to let my work movin' on </br>
</br>

# Part 1

I started digging, investigating all the hardwares below, </br>
Also researching its already-known boot flow </br>
To get a mainline kernel and free software running strong, </br>
Even though manufacturer's supports are all gone </br>

# Chorus

So tonight, its another "Reversing Time", </br>
Grab a programmer, capture logs you've missed </br>
'Cause we're all forced to join such endless fight, </br>
To take back rights we no longer have </br>
At "Spacewar nights" </br>
</br> 

# Part 2

When logs on screen begin to scroll, </br>
Crashdump screams and (kernel) panics show, </br>
You know it's time to patch my progress more </br>
Until a small framebuffer starts to grow,
The-eight-Tuxes finished their unique show </br>
No proprietary blobs, no closed-source chains</br>
</br>

To guard my privacy and my personal digital rights, </br>
I kept the flame of free software sights, </br>
Now Arch runs smooth and Glyph lights are dancing free, </br>
On a phone they meant to slowly fade away </br>
</br>

# Chorus 
'Cause there is a day, I'll leave this world behind </br>
So grab a programmer and compile your unique life </br>
We’re the ones who still refuse to kneel, </br>
Those hard nights will never die, </br>
At Spacewar nights </br>
</br>

# Ending

One day these closed phones will all run free, </br>
Those hard nights will never die, </br>
Called "Spacewar nights"</br>
</br>
